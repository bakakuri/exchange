    Built to be fair

    These rules are enforced by the server, so nobody has to take anyone's word for it.

    • Proof before payment

      Credits move only after the campaign's creator approves your proof.

    • Budgets are held, not lost

      Launching a campaign holds its budget. Anything unspent comes back if you cancel.

    • No paying yourself

      You can't complete your own tasks, so every follow comes from someone else.

    • One reward per person

      Each member can complete a task once, so the numbers stay honest.
